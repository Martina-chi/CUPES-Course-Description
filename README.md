# CUPES Course Description — Master Version

Course-description master library for **Yutong Chi (迟雨桐)**, Bachelor of Education in **Social Sports Guidance and Management** at **Capital University of Physical Education and Sports (CUPES)**.

This repository is adapted from the original TJU Typst course-description template and is being rebuilt for European master's applications.

## Document principles

- Course inclusion is based on the student's confirmed academic records / credit-recognition records rather than on the curriculum catalogue alone.
- Official CUPES curriculum descriptions are used as the primary source for course content, course codes and teaching hours where applicable.
- Grades are intentionally omitted from the Course Description document.
- The ECTS-equivalent calculation used in this application document is **1 CUPES credit = 1.5 ECTS**.
- Original CUPES credits and teaching hours remain visible so that admissions institutions can conduct their own equivalency assessment if required.
- Where a curriculum module spans several semesters, only transcript-confirmed completed credits are presented as completed coursework.

## Current structure

- `main.typ` — core research, management, society/community and sport-science courses.
- `additional_courses.typ` — sport studies, education, Olympic studies, fitness, sport practice, specialization and supporting academic/business courses.
- `template.typ` — CUPES-specific document layout, credit/workload statement and course table.
- `IMG_1040.png` — CUPES logo currently used on the cover.

## Credit and workload context

The official 2021 curriculum states a standard programme duration of four years and a minimum graduation requirement of 171 CUPES credits. The curriculum contains 147 credits of classroom coursework and 24 credits of centralized practical components, with 2,998 classroom teaching hours in total.

For this application-document library, ECTS-equivalent figures are calculated consistently at **1.5 ECTS per CUPES credit**. This presentation does not replace the original CUPES credit values or an admissions institution's own formal equivalency assessment.

## Intended use

`main.typ` is the master source. School-specific application versions can later select the courses most relevant to each programme's prerequisite areas, for example:

- sport management / sport business / sport organizations;
- sport studies / sport and society / international and Olympic studies;
- research methodology and empirical research;
- sport science and exercise science;
- coaching, instruction and practical sport competence.
